Get Advice

If someone is bullying you online, remember these steps:

1. Don't argue

Stay calm. You don't have to reply to hurtful messages.

2. Save the evidence

Take screenshots of harmful messages, comments, or posts.

3. Block the sender

Block the person so they cannot continue contacting you through that account.

4. Report the content

Report bullying messages or posts to the social media platform.

5. Tell a trusted adult

Talk to a parent, teacher, school psychologist, or another adult you trust.

Find Help

You do not have to face cyberbullying alone. You can ask these people for help.

School Psychologist

A school psychologist can listen to you and help you deal with difficult feelings.

Trusted Teacher

Talk to a teacher you trust. They may help you find a safe solution.

Parents or Guardians

Tell a trusted family member what is happening and ask for support.

Support Organizations

You can contact a local child protection or youth support organization for advice.
